Privacy Policy for TimeoPro

Last Updated: January 29, 2026

TimeoPro ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application TimeoPro (the "App").

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

We may collect personal information that you provide directly to us when using the App, including:

  • Name and contact information (email address, phone number)
  • Business information (business name, address, type)
  • Member/customer information (names, contact details, membership status)
  • Transaction and billing information
  • Payment information (processed securely, not stored by us)

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you use the App, we may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Device information (device type, operating system, unique device identifiers)
  • Usage data (app features used, time spent, interactions)
  • Log data (IP address, access times, app crashes)

1.3 Camera and QR Code Data

The App requires camera access for:

  • Scanning QR codes for member check-ins
  • Capturing member photos (with consent)

We do not store camera images except when explicitly saved by the user for member profiles.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Provide Services: To operate and maintain the App's core functionality (member management, billing, invoicing, orders)
  • Business Operations: To process transactions, generate invoices, and manage memberships
  • Communication: To send you invoices, receipts, and important service notifications
  • Improve Services: To analyze usage patterns and improve App performance
  • Support: To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • Security: To detect, prevent, and address technical issues and security threats
  • Compliance: To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Where We Store Your Data

Your data is securely stored using Convex (https://convex.dev), a cloud-based backend service. Convex infrastructure is hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS) with data centers in the United States.

3.2 Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your data:

  • End-to-end encryption for data transmission
  • Encrypted data storage at rest
  • Secure authentication and authorization
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Access controls and monitoring

However, please note: No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

4.1 We Do NOT Sell Your Data

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

4.2 We May Share Data With:

  • Service Providers: Third-party vendors who help us operate the App (Convex for backend, SMTP services for email)
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, sale, or acquisition of all or a portion of our business
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or government authority
  • Protection: To protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our users

4.3 Your Business Data

Data you create about your business operations (members, transactions, invoices) is NOT shared with other TimeoPro users. Each business account is isolated and secure.

5. Your Data Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

5.1 Access and Portability

You can access your data anytime within the App. You may request a copy of your data in a portable format by contacting us.

5.2 Correction

You can update or correct your information directly in the App settings.

5.3 Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at support@timeopro.com. We will delete your data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

5.4 Opt-Out

You can opt-out of non-essential communications by updating your notification preferences in the App.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as:

  • Your account is active
  • Needed to provide you services
  • Required to comply with legal obligations
  • Necessary to resolve disputes and enforce agreements

Transaction and invoice data may be retained for up to 7 years for tax and accounting purposes, as required by law.

7. Children's Privacy

TimeoPro is a business management tool intended for use by adults (18+ years old). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.

8. International Users

TimeoPro is operated in and provided from the United States. If you are accessing the App from outside the United States, please be aware that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States where our servers and Convex infrastructure are located.

By using the App, you consent to the transfer of your information to the United States and the processing of your data in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable U.S. laws.

9. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

9.1 Convex (Backend & Database)

We use Convex (https://convex.dev) to store and manage app data. Convex Privacy Policy: https://convex.dev/privacy

9.2 SMTP Email Services

We may use third-party SMTP services (such as SMTP2GO) to send invoice emails. These services only process email delivery and do not store your data.

9.3 Cloudflare Workers (Optional)

If you enable PDF invoice generation, we use Cloudflare Workers to convert invoices to PDF format. Cloudflare Privacy Policy: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/

10. Cookies and Tracking

The App does not use cookies as it is a native mobile application. We may collect anonymous usage analytics to improve app performance and user experience.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy in the App
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Sending you an email notification (for significant changes)

Your continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

12. Your Consent

By using TimeoPro, you consent to:

  • The collection and use of your information as described in this Privacy Policy
  • The storage of your data on Convex cloud infrastructure
  • The transfer of your data to the United States
